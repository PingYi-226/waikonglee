disclosure

Open an abstract: only one stays open (a group). Click twice quickly: it turns round. Close it from the button inside: focus returns to the trigger. The card below starts open.

  • High Throughput Lattice-Based Signatures on GPUs: Comparing Falcon and Mitaka
    The US National Institute of Standards and Technology initiated a standardization process for post-quantum cryptography. Falcon was selected for its compact signatures; Mitaka is a simpler variant that is easier to parallelize. This work implements both on GPUs and compares their throughput for signing and verification.
  • Speed Record of AES-CTR and AES-ECB Bit-Sliced Implementation on GPUs
    Bit-slicing packs many AES states into machine words so that one logical operation processes all of them. On GPUs the technique reaches a new speed record for the counter and electronic-codebook modes while staying constant-time.
  • QuripfeNet: Quantum-Resistant IPFE-Based Neural Network
    Inner-product functional encryption lets a server evaluate the first layer of a network on encrypted inputs. QuripfeNet builds it from lattice assumptions, so that it stays secure against quantum adversaries, and accelerates it on GPUs.
